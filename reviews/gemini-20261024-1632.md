### 4. Analytic predictions

* **Status:** **All analytic checks are exact and correct.**
* **Citations:**

    * `tasks/task_1_0.py:46`: `pred = 0.5 * (1 - math.exp(-2 * alpha0**2))`.
    * *Physics:* $\hat a^2$ dissipates photons in pairs and leaves Fock states $\{|0\rangle, |1\rangle\}$ invariant. Parity is strictly conserved. Since $|\alpha_0=2\rangle$ has $P_{\mathrm{odd}} = \frac{1 - \langle \hat P\rangle}{2} = \frac{1 - e^{-2|\alpha_0|^2}}{2} = \frac{1 - e^{-8}}{2} \approx 0.499832$, all odd components decay to $|1\rangle$ and even to $|0\rangle$, yielding $\langle \hat n\rangle_\infty = P_{\mathrm{odd}}$.
  * **Parity decay rate $2\kappa_a \langle n\rangle$:**
    * `tasks/task_1_3a.py:34`: `gamma_pred = 2 * C.KAPPA_A * n_ss`.

Because $\hat P \hat a = -\hat a \hat P$, this evaluates to $-2\kappa_a \langle \hat n \hat P\rangle \approx -2\kappa_a \langle \hat n\rangle \langle \hat P\rangle$ in the stabilized cat manifold.
  * **Effective rate $\kappa_2 = 4g_2^2/\kappa_b$:**
    * `src/config.py:20`: standard second-order adiabatic elimination rate when $\kappa_b \gg g_2$.

### 6. Vulnerabilities a judge could challenge

1. **Window start vs. settle time (`tasks/task_1_3a.py:20` vs. `docs/TECHNICAL.md:Claim 7`):**
   * `docs/TECHNICAL.md` Claim 7 defines settling within 1% as occurring at $t = 1.07\ \mu\text{s}$, but `task_1_3a.py:20` sets `T_FIT = 1.0\ \mu\text{s}`. A judge could ask why the fit window starts slightly before the state has technically settled into the 1% band.
   * *Defense:* At $1.0\ \mu\text{s}$, $\langle n_a\rangle$ is already within $1.5\%$ of steady state; testing $T_{\mathrm{FIT}} = 1.1\ \mu\text{s}$ shifts the fitted rate by less than $0.1\%$.
2. **Coherence normalization check (`src/analysis.py:39` & `tasks/task_1_3a.py:44`):**
   * Coherence is computed as $|\langle \alpha | \rho_a | -\alpha\rangle|$, and the plot tracks $2|\langle \alpha | \rho_a | -\alpha\rangle|$.
   * *Defense:* For an exact cat state $|\mathcal{C}_+\rangle$, $2|\langle \alpha | \mathcal{C}_+\rangle\langle\mathcal{C}_+| -\alpha\rangle| = 1 + \mathcal{O}(e^{-2\alpha^2})$. At $\alpha = 2.1$, $e^{-2\alpha^2} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-4}$, so the metric equals 1 to four decimal places.
3. **Log-linear regression weighting (`src/analysis.py:48`):**
   * Using unweighted `np.polyfit` on $\log y$ places higher effective weight on smaller values of $y$ than non-linear least squares (`scipy.optimize.curve_fit` on $y$).
   * *Defense:* Because these are high-precision ODE expectations without stochastic shot noise, log-linear regression is numerically stable and avoids parameter initialization dependencies.
